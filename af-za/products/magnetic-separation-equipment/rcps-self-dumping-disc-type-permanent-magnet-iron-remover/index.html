Product FAQ

Questions to resolve before ordering

Self-cleaning is normally considered where ferrous contamination must be discharged continuously. Manual cleaning may be appropriate when contamination is low and the line can be isolated safely for service.

When is self-cleaning preferred over manual cleaning?

Self-cleaning is normally considered where ferrous contamination must be discharged continuously. Manual cleaning may be appropriate when contamination is low and the line can be isolated safely for service.

Should the magnet be installed inline or cross-belt?

The installation direction depends on belt speed, burden depth, the discharge side, available headroom and the required exposure to the magnetic field. The conveyor arrangement should be reviewed before selection.

What information is needed for a suspended magnet quote?

Please provide belt width, speed, material layer depth, suspension height, material description, the largest expected tramp item, desired cleaning method and a layout drawing or photos when available.

Can this separator remove all metals?

No. A magnetic separator is used for ferromagnetic contaminants. Non-ferrous metals and non-magnetic stainless steel require a different separation or detection stage.
