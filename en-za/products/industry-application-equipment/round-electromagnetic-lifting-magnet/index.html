Product FAQ

Questions buyers ask

Answers apply to this product family and do not replace a project-specific engineering review.

What information is needed to select a lifting magnet?

Provide the actual ferromagnetic load shape and condition, handling method, maximum duty, lifting arrangement, power availability and site safety requirements.

Can a lifting magnet handle any material?

No. It is selected for defined ferromagnetic materials and duty conditions. The load and surface condition must be reviewed before a configuration is confirmed.

Are lifting safety requirements part of the product page?

The page identifies the need for a controlled lifting plan, but final safety procedure and site compliance are the responsibility of the operating project and qualified personnel.

Can a catalog page confirm lifting capacity?

No. Capacity and safety suitability require the exact configuration, load condition and verified documentation.
