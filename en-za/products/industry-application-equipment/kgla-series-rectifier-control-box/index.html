Product FAQ

Questions buyers ask

Answers apply to this product family and do not replace a project-specific engineering review.

Can an explosion-protection rating be selected from the product name?

No. Any classification, certificate and installation requirement must be verified against the exact equipment, documents and site conditions.

What must be confirmed before a control box is quoted?

The connected equipment, supply, control mode, required interlocks, installation environment and project documentation should all be supplied for review.

Is a control box a substitute for a magnetic separator?

No. It supports or controls a defined equipment duty. The magnetic separation equipment and the control requirement are selected separately.

Who confirms final site compliance?

Final site compliance must be confirmed by the responsible project, electrical and safety teams using the verified documentation for the supplied configuration.
