Selection checklist

What to send for an initial configuration review

  • State whether the objective is recovery, upgrading, cleaning or tramp iron removal.
  • For wet circuits, describe the slurry condition and available water or process constraints.
  • Share laboratory, plant or sample information when available.
  • No recovery or grade claim is made without representative material and process evidence.
  • Magnetic separation does not replace required crushing, screening, classification or other upstream preparation stages.

Product FAQ

Questions to resolve before ordering

Selection begins with the mineral, particle size, feed condition, desired magnetic and non-magnetic product streams, throughput and the place of the machine in the process flow.

How is a mineral magnetic separator selected?

Selection begins with the mineral, particle size, feed condition, desired magnetic and non-magnetic product streams, throughput and the place of the machine in the process flow.

Can one separator solve every ore separation problem?

No. Magnetic response, liberation, feed preparation and separation target vary by material. A dry, wet, high-gradient or drum configuration is selected for the actual duty.

What should be sent for an initial technical review?

Provide mineral name, feed size, moisture or slurry condition, throughput, process sketch, desired product streams and any available test or plant data.

Are recovery results guaranteed from a catalog page?

No. Recovery depends on the material and process. Results should only be discussed after the feed and operating conditions are reviewed.
