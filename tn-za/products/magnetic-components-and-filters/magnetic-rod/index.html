Magnetic Rod

Selection support for Magnetic Rod in South Africa and African mining and industrial markets.

Product Overview

Magnetic Rod is reviewed for removing ferrous contamination from a gravity-fed, pneumatic or pipeline material stream after the material and cleaning requirements are confirmed.

Why this configuration

Confirm the material flow direction, connection dimensions, pressure or temperature conditions, access for cleaning and hygiene or process constraints where relevant.

Available options

  • Magnetic element arrangement
  • Housing and connection configuration
  • Manual or automatic cleaning by model

How it works

The material passes through a selected magnetic element arrangement that captures ferrous contamination. The housing and cleaning method depend on the verified model.

Typical applications and materials

  • Bulk material handling
  • Mineral processing
  • Recycling

How to select

  • Material type, particle size and flow condition
  • Gravity, pneumatic or pipeline installation
  • Connection dimensions and available space
  • Cleaning frequency and contamination level
  • Material or contaminant to be removed
  • Process capacity and particle or lump size
  • Available installation space and maintenance access
  • Dust, moisture, temperature and corrosion exposure
