Selection checklist

What to send for an initial configuration review

  • State whether ferrous material has already been removed upstream.
  • Provide the screened feed size, feed rate and material mix.
  • Describe the downstream collection or sorting stage so the equipment role is clear.
  • Recovery performance is not stated without a defined feed and a line review.
  • The product does not replace upstream screening, controlled feed or ferrous pre-separation where those stages are required.

Product FAQ

Questions to resolve before ordering

A magnetic pre-separation stage is commonly reviewed before non-ferrous sorting so the feed reaching the eddy current stage is prepared for its intended duty.

Why is ferrous removal checked before an eddy current separator?

A magnetic pre-separation stage is commonly reviewed before non-ferrous sorting so the feed reaching the eddy current stage is prepared for its intended duty.

Can an eddy current separator remove ferrous tramp iron?

No. Eddy current equipment is used for non-ferrous metal recovery. Ferromagnetic material should be addressed with the appropriate magnetic separation stage.

What information is needed for recycling sorting selection?

Please share feed size, material mix, throughput, upstream preparation, existing ferrous removal, required output fractions and the available conveyor layout.

Can a stainless-steel sorting conveyor be described as a general metal separator?

No. Its application depends on the actual equipment capability and feed. It should be specified separately from magnetic and eddy-current stages.

GJT Window Metal Detector

The GJT window metal detector is an inspection device installed around a defined material path. It is used to detect a metal signal under the conditions set by the actual opening size, conveyed material, product effect and line configuration. A detector is not represented as a magnetic separator or as an automatic removal device: the required alarm, stop or reject response must be specified with the conveyor line.
