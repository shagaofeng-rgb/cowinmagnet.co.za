Metal Detection & Recycling Sorting

GJT Window Metal Detector

The GJT window metal detector is an inspection device installed around a defined material path. It is used to detect a metal signal under the conditions set by the actual opening size, conveyed material, product effect and line configuration. A detector is not represented as a magnetic separator or as an automatic removal device: the required alarm, stop or reject response must be specified with the conveyor line.

  • Detection is not removal
  • Opening and material matter
  • Integration is part of selection
  • Magnetic separation can be complementary

Selection checklist

What to send for an initial configuration review

  • Describe the actual conveyed product and operating conditions.
  • State whether the line requires an alarm, stop signal or a separately designed reject action.
  • Do not use a metal detector specification as proof that metal is automatically removed.
  • Detection capability must not be claimed without the actual material, aperture and operating condition.
  • The detector is not presented as an automatic metal-removal device.

What does a window metal detector do?

It monitors material passing through a defined aperture and provides a response according to its configuration. The final detection capability depends on the actual product, opening and operating conditions.

Does a metal detector remove metal automatically?

Not by itself. Removal requires an appropriate reject, stop or downstream separation arrangement designed for the production line.

Can a magnetic separator and metal detector be used together?

Yes, when their separate roles are clear: magnetic equipment addresses ferromagnetic material, while a detector provides the detection function required by the line.

What is required for a detector selection review?

Provide the window size, conveyor arrangement, material, product effect, expected contaminant, belt speed, required response and available electrical interface.
